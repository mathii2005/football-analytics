# Match dashboard (dashboard/src)

Scope: the whole dashboard app. Mode: Operate. Users: head coach (laptop, day after), coaches (phone, before training), analyst. Job: from a finding to the Veo moment in one click. Tabs: Aperçu, En profondeur, Terrain, Clips Veo. French.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every tab opens on a black scoreboard band carrying the 4–6 numbers that matter, in gold; the evidence sits below on white. Refuses the generic grid of equal KPI cards.

OWN-WORLD: White ground, black #0a0a0a band and ink, Lauréats gold #c79741 only for numbers that matter, the active tab and video actions; hairline rules; tabular figures; one workhorse sans; no gradients, no glass, no shadows beyond one level.

STORY: The coach sees the score and the few numbers that summarise the match, reads 3–5 key points, then drills into a tab or straight into the clips worth reviewing.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Black band full width: logo/wordmark, "Lauréats 2–1 Vanier", date/venue, tabs; below it, inside the band, a row of 5 large gold figures with small labels. Under the band on white: key points (left, 1/3) and possession timeline (right, 2/3). Primary action: "Voir les clips" in gold.

FORM: Tableau d'affichage (stadium scoreboard), dealt position 6 of 7, seed 8a5e13d7. Signature interaction: every clickable moment opens Veo 8 s early; hover/focus on timeline segments shows the possession.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
