# Match dashboard (dashboard/src)

Scope: the whole dashboard app. Mode: Operate. Users: head coach (laptop, day after), coaches (phone, before training), analyst. Job: every stat the tagging supports, each leading to its Veo moments. Tabs: Aperçu, Possession, Attaque, Terrain, Clips Veo. French. Dense and technical by user decision (2026-09-30).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every tab opens on a black scoreboard band carrying its 5 key numbers in gold; the evidence sits below on white, dense and technical, and every countable stat opens its clips. Refuses the generic grid of equal KPI cards.

OWN-WORLD: White ground, black #0a0a0a band and ink, Lauréats gold #c79741 only for numbers that matter, positives and actions (deep gold #85601a on white); losses in grey; action types in four ink steps; hairline rules; tabular figures; Barlow Condensed + Barlow; no gradients, no glass, no shadows beyond one level.

STORY: The coach reads the score and key figures, the summary and momentum, then drills into possession, attack or pitch views, and clicks any count to review the moments.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Black band: wordmark, "Lauréats 2–1 Vanier", date/venue, gold "Voir les clips", five tabs; inside the band a row of 5 large gold figures. Under it on white: key points (1/3) and momentum chart (2/3); possession timeline full width below.

FORM: Tableau d'affichage (stadium scoreboard), dealt position 6 of 7, seed 8a5e13d7. Signature interaction: any count (zone, funnel stage, counter-press zone, set pieces) opens the clip library pre-filtered; every clip opens Veo 8 s early.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
